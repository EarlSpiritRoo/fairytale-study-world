How to move ✦

Fablenook Guide

  • Hold the left mouse button on the ground and your character walks toward the cursor. Let go to stop. A quick click walks to that spot.
  • WASD or arrow keys also walk (hold Shift to stroll). Any key cancels a click-walk.
  • Look around: right-drag, or two-finger drag on a trackpad. Zoom: scroll wheel, or pinch on a trackpad.
  • On a phone: press and hold the ground to walk toward your finger, or use the joystick that appears under your left thumb. Drag with a second finger, or on empty sky, to look.
  • F or Enter (or the pink prompt button) talks to characters and uses objects.
  • Camera: drag the sky (or right-drag) to orbit freely, or drag the knob of the round camera pad in the bottom-right corner. Q / E make quick 45° turns. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Solo timer: press "Start studying", pick 25 or 50 minutes or your own length, and add a subject if you like. A 📖 bubble shows everyone you are busy. It keeps counting if you switch tabs.
  • Study Circles: every realm has one. Walk in and stand still: you sit down and join the shared timer. If nobody has started a round, you choose the length for everyone.
  • After each focus round the whole circle gets a 5-minute break together, then anyone can start the next round.
  • You earn only the minutes you were actually in the circle. Your total shows beside your name.
  • Focus mode: while a session runs, chat is emotes only. 👋 ✨ 💪 ☕
  • There are five realms: Skyfield, Coral Lagoon, Hollyhollow, Moonlit Pavilion and Petalwood.
  • Gates: each realm has four glowing gates near where you arrive. Walk through one.
  • Map: the ✦ button lists every realm, how many people are there and how many are studying, with a Travel button.
  • You only see the people who are in the same realm as you.
  • Actions (💃 button, or keys 1 to 6): Stand, Sit, Dance, Wave, Spin, Cheer. Everyone nearby sees them. Moving cancels an action.
  • Snapshots (📸 button): takes a picture of the world in a little frame with the realm's name. Tick "Show name tags" if you want names in the shot.
  • On a computer you can download the picture or copy it. On a phone you can share it straight to your apps, or save it.
  • Sound: the ⚙️ menu has Ambience on / off, separate Ambience and Effects volume sliders, an optional Calm pad, and Mute all sound. All remembered on this device. Sounds like waterfalls, the fireplace and the lake shore get louder as you walk closer.
